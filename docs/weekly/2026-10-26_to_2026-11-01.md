# Weekly Technical Record: 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-01

## 1. Technical uncertainty

The corrected direct PSG detector remained weak on external data. It was uncertain whether that weakness arose from the rare-event formulation itself and whether an established stage-first formulation could preserve more transportable REM-to-Wake structure.

## 2. Hypothesis and fixed comparison

`H-STAGE-FIRST-XDATA` proposed that causal five-stage prediction followed by deterministic REM-to-Wake derivation would outperform corrected direct `ADV3-CHANGE`. The protocol was frozen on October 25 before fitting. Work resumed and the result package was completed on October 31.

The experiment reused the corrected 196-night, 130-participant-group PSG cohort, verified 444-value causal features, leave-one-dataset-out training, fixed balanced multinomial logistic regression, and two deterministic event rules. gx36m and ISRUC group 3 remained the primary external scope. No threshold, lag, tolerance, or run length was selected from the held-out results.

## 3. Execution failure and correction

The first execution exposed a wrong expected-row constant: the retained pre-fit class counts sum to 372,361, not the transcribed 367,697. The model and score artifacts contained 372,361 rows and were unchanged. The runner invariant was corrected to derive the expected value from the retained class-count table.

All three fits also reached the frozen 1,000-iteration limit. This is a scientific/numerical outcome, not an execution-control failure. The runner was corrected to report non-convergence in the hypothesis gate rather than suppress otherwise finite result tables. No solver, tolerance, feature, label, prediction, or event rule changed.

## 4. Primary result

Primary pooled external `STAGE-CONFIRM2` reached precision 0.0896, recall 0.2137, F1 0.1263, and 0.4523 false alarms/hour. Corrected direct `ADV3-CHANGE` reached precision 0.2069, recall 0.2564, F1 0.2290, and 0.2048 false alarms/hour.

The F1 difference was -0.1027. Its participant-stratified bootstrap 95% interval was -0.1859 to -0.0125. The stage-first false-alarm difference was +0.2475/hour, with interval +0.1203 to +0.3551/hour. Both primary directions were adverse.

On ISRUC group 3, both stage-first rules produced zero true positives. `STAGE-CONFIRM2` F1 was 0.1441 on gx36m and 0 on ISRUC, so the formulation did not transport across the two external datasets.

## 5. Failure diagnostic

A post-result diagnostic plan was frozen before the mechanism analysis. It prohibited refitting and new candidate creation.

Predicted all-stage transition rates were 1.790 to 2.928 times human rates. Predicted REM-bout counts were 4.607 to 7.520 times human counts, while median predicted REM duration collapsed to 30-45 seconds versus human medians of 120-1,050 seconds.

The exact `REM, REM, W, W` pattern appeared at 41/365 BOAS, 20/85 gx36m, and 0/32 ISRUC references. On ISRUC, `STAGE-CONFIRM2` had no alarm within +/-120 seconds of any reference. This identifies sequence fragmentation and external timing failure rather than a failure caused only by the primary `[0,+15]`-second match interval.

## 6. Decision

The stage-first hypothesis is rejected for v0.1. The corrected direct formulation remains stronger, but neither approach is reliable enough for wearable or clinical use. The Block 11 streaming `no_go` is unchanged.

No post-result convergence, smoothing, lag, confirmation-length, threshold, or tolerance tuning is authorized on these outcomes. A future temporal stage model requires a new protocol and fresh evaluation data.
