## 1. Technical uncertainty

Earlier train diagnostics found much lower REM-to-Wake recall for the 53 clean primary events than for the 127 MAD-flagged primary events. It was uncertain whether conventional standardization and unequal positive-tier representation biased the BLSTM-CRF score.

## 2. Hypothesis and controls

A protocol was committed before implementation. The experimental candidate retained the exact BLSTM-CRF, ten features, eight-epoch context, folds, seeds, optimizer, 60 epochs, threshold grid, full-night scoring, and event evaluator.

Only two mechanisms changed together: median/IQR scaling and weights that assigned half of total positive loss to clean events and half to MAD-flagged events. Advancement required at least +0.05 overall F1, +0.10 clean recall, and no increase in false alarms/hour.
