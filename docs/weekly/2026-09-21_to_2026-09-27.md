## 1. Technological Uncertainty

The eight-epoch wearable logistic detector represented temporal context as 80 unrelated columns and remained low precision. Cross-view consensus had previously shown that structured agreement can suppress false alarms, but required PSG. The unresolved question was whether wearable-only learned temporal ordering and an explicit event-state sequence could reproduce that specificity mechanism.

## 2. Frozen Method

The protocol was committed on September 19 before result-producing implementation. It fixed:

- one small bidirectional LSTM plus three-state linear-chain CRF;
- the same 2,743 reviewed train candidates and stored wearable bandpower features used by the direct baseline;
- a matched flattened logistic comparator;
- the five existing participant-grouped train folds;
- 60 fixed training epochs without early stopping or search;
- full-night out-of-fold event scoring and separate 99-point thresholds;
- a required F1 gain of at least 0.05 with no false-alarm increase; and
- complete validation/test closure.

## 3. Execution Failure and Correction

The first completed run was rejected when an over-broad path check matched `validation` in the name of the historical train-feature directory. Its full outputs were retained. The predicate was narrowed to explicit validation/test path segments; no input, model, threshold, metric, or hypothesis changed.

The corrected run was accepted on September 22. All external fold models and full-night scores reproduced their stored hashes.

## 4. Primary Result

| Model | Threshold | Precision | Recall | F1 | False alarms/hour |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| LR-OOF | 0.98 | 0.0581 | 0.3167 | 0.0982 | 1.4679 |
| LC-1 | 0.99 | 0.1436 | 0.3111 | 0.1965 | 0.5306 |

`LC-1` improved F1 by 0.0983 and reduced false alarms by 0.9373/hour. It produced 334 false positives versus 924 for the logistic comparator, while true positives were nearly unchanged at 56 versus 57. The result supports improved temporal specificity rather than improved sensitivity.

## 5. Participant Evidence

The paired participant bootstrap F1-difference interval was +0.0485 to +0.1367. The false-alarm-difference interval was -1.4804 to -0.4089/hour. Both directions exclude zero, but the F1 lower bound is slightly below the predefined +0.05 materiality gate. The direction is participant-supported; the full material-size gate remains point-supported.

## 7. Decision

`LC-1` is eligible for a new locked or external wearable confirmation protocol. It does not replace a frozen model and must not be evaluated on the already used validation or current test partitions. F1 remains below 0.20 in this train-development estimate, so the result is promising method evidence rather than application readiness.

The combined comparison cannot isolate the LSTM contribution from the CRF contribution. No retrospective ablation is added. Block 9 external-PSG compatibility remains the next scheduled block and is not a wearable confirmation cohort.
