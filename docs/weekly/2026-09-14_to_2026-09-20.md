## 4. Failed and Inconclusive Approaches Retained

- Severe both-channel noise yielded no true detection.
- Isolated `HB_1` noise reduced F1 by 0.0704 and increased false alarms by 3.0058/hour.
- Train-only augmentation reduced that induced false-alarm failure but decreased clean F1 by 0.0099 and therefore failed the clean-advancement gate.
- Fixed PSG/wearable OR reduced F1 by 0.0530 and increased false alarms by 1.1358/hour.
- Fixed 2-of-3 laboratory consensus reduced false alarms consistently, but its F1-difference interval crossed zero and it requires PSG.

These outcomes stop further Block 8 variations rather than motivating post-result threshold or rule changes.

## 5. Knowledge Gained

The existing wearable detector is not robust enough to advance. Its failure is structured by channel identity and degradation type, and repairing one controlled failure does not improve clean event separability. Agreement across signal views contains a specificity mechanism, but the existing demonstration is not wearable-only.

The boundary-tolerance gain of 0.1081 had a participant lower bound of 0.0238. This makes coarse stage-derived timing the prioritized later uncertainty while preserving the distinction between improved matching tolerance and improved detector precision.
